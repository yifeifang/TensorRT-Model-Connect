# Model and input-asset license audit

Reviewed September 11, 2026 against the showcase v2.2 inputs and pinned official sources. This is an engineering distribution decision with evidence, not a warranty that all possible uses or third-party rights have been cleared. Native libraries, SDKs, Electron and build tools require their separate component review.

## Release decision

Submit application and bridge source, original prompts, provenance, license notices and installation/build scripts. **Do not include model checkpoints, compiled bundles, caches, native SDK binaries or the current development distribution directory in the source PR.** Users obtain selected models from their official owners and build locally. This is a conservative delivery choice: most selected models permit redistribution when their conditions are met.

Use the maintainer-owned vehicle photograph as the redistributable built-in photo. Exclude the existing indoor photograph and generated/external WAV files from the source payload. Retain original sample text and provide optional local synthesis or microphone/import input. Do not silently download externally hosted media whose separate license remains unresolved.

An install script does not override a license. A published container containing weights, SDK libraries or an existing cache also distributes those materials, including files left in earlier image layers. The initial release should publish source/build instructions with empty caches and user-controlled downloads, rather than a preloaded GPU container.

Apache-2.0 permits source/object distribution subject to its license, attribution, applicable NOTICE and modification-notice conditions; it does not grant general trademark rights. Compiling or changing precision is not a reason to drop those obligations. MIT likewise requires its copyright and permission notice. [Apache-2.0 sections 4 and 6](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0), [Whisper MIT license](https://github.com/openai/whisper/blob/main/LICENSE).

[OpenMDW-1.1](https://github.com/OpenMDW/OpenMDW/blob/main/1.1/LICENSE.OpenMDW-1.1) permits redistribution when the agreement and applicable notices of copyright/origin accompany it. It imposes no additional conditions on generated outputs, while expressly leaving third-party rights clearance with the user. This does not grant rights in an unrelated input recording or photo.

The [NVIDIA Open Model License](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/agreements/enterprise-software/nvidia-open-model-license/) inspected on this date states last modified October 24, 2025. It allows commercial use and redistribution subject to its conditions. Model copies need the agreement and the exact Notice: **Licensed by NVIDIA Corporation under the NVIDIA Open Model License**. It incorporates [Trustworthy AI terms](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/agreements/trustworthy-ai/terms/), guardrail-related conditions, separate-component terms, and applicable trade requirements. Its Cosmos-specific branding condition does not apply merely because a tokenizer is from another model family. Keep realtime voice optional in an installer and present both of its agreements before acquisition.

The older Qwen 0.6B, 1.7B and Instruct-2507 experiments are not selected capabilities. Exclude those development artifacts and their outputs. Changing a manifest to another model requires another license/provenance review.

## Installer and review requirements

1. Resolve models only from the official repository and exact revision in the inventory. Preserve checkpoint/config/tokenizer hashes, model card, relevant license text, any NOTICE, and a local installation receipt. License acknowledgement does not replace license compliance.
2. Build user-owned bundles locally. Keep checkpoints, native caches, and downloaded legal documents outside the source archive. For the older reused voice bundle, a family manifest pin is not complete chain-of-custody evidence for every byte; use a fresh pinned acquisition/build for an external release.
3. Keep mandatory third-party identities in legal files and technical records. Generic English capability labels may remain in the application without erasing legally required attribution.
4. Ensure both the source payload and packaged file manifest exclude unresolved media, caches, weights and raw development logs. Screenshots and exported outputs can still embed excluded inputs.
5. A distributable binary or container is a separate release gate: audit its exact file/layer inventory, provide required notices and verify SDK redistribution permissions. The ability to build it locally is not evidence that every resulting binary may be uploaded.

The recorded model revisions and declarations are sufficient to implement a transparent user-download workflow. They do not clear every training-data claim, every output, or a future substituted component. Unresolved media and preloaded binary distribution remain excluded from the initial source contribution.
